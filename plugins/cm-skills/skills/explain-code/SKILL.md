When explaining code, always include:

1. **Draw a diagram**: Use ASCII art to show the flow, structure, or relationships
2. **Walk through the code**: Explain step-by-step what happens
3. **Highlight a gotcha**: What's a common mistake or misconception?
4. **Offer an improvement to be made**: Suggest a way to enhance the code, or a best practice to follow.

Keep explanations conversational. For complex concepts, please feel free to offer analogies to make them easier to understand, as well as to break down the explanation into smaller, digestible parts for a possible refactor.
